Made from scratch. Served with a story.

Teri Peralta learned to cook at her grandmother's side. By 1993 she was serving food at Cattleman's and driving school buses before sunrise.

In 2008 she opened Coyote Café with her grandmother's salsa recipe and a simple idea: honest food, generous portions, and a welcome that feels like family.

The recipe is still secret. The welcome never changed.

  • 1993Teri starts in restaurants — and behind the wheel of a school bus.
  • 2008Coyote Café opens on California City Blvd.
  • TodaySame salsa. Same cook. Same small-town welcome.
